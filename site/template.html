Four tools in one window

Pattern test

15 full-screen patterns at the display's own resolution, one image pixel per screen pixel: SMPTE colour bars after RP 219 with their sub-patterns, solid colours for dead and stuck pixels, gray ramps and steps for banding, black and white level patches, a grid for geometry and checkerboards for sharpness.

Motion test

A square or circle moves across the screen at 5 to 240 steps per second, pixel by pixel or shape by shape. Look for blur, trails and uneven steps; the bar shows the display's refresh rate and the rate the app really draws at.

Pixel refresh

Random red, green, blue, white and black cells, up to 60 times a second, in a window you move over the stuck pixel or across the whole display. It can bring a stuck pixel back; a dead one cannot be fixed.

Display information

Every monitor with its name, graphics adapter, resolution and scale, refresh rate, colour depth, physical size, diagonal, pixels per inch and aspect ratios, and the display modes it supports. Run any test full screen on any monitor, in any of its modes.
